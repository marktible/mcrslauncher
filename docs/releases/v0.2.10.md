# MCRS Launcher 0.2.10

The launcher has a cleaner layout, simpler navigation, and clearer controls for playing and managing profiles.

- Replace numbered, outlined navigation with compact icons and a clear active-page highlight.
- Give Play one primary game action and keep account selection, offline profiles, Microsoft sign-in, and Offline mode together in the player panel.
- Refine the landscape banner, card spacing, colors, and typography; simplify the update shortcut and keep game-folder access beside the game.
- Group Settings into General, Game updates, Native mod builds, Player profiles, and Storage & support. Simplify the mod import form and empty state.
- Keep compact navigation and scrolling for small windows, with truncation and tooltips for long installed-build names.

## Flatpak signing key
